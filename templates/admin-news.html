Connect Gmail

"Connect Gmail" makes the site send its emails from your own Gmail. Once, before the first connect: in Google Cloud (the project of the Google login) open APIs & Services → Library → Gmail API → Enable. On Google's page pick your Gmail; if Google says it has not verified the app, press Advanced → Go to …, then tick "Send email on your behalf".
