We collect information you provide directly, including your email address when you create an account or sign in with Google OAuth. We also collect usage data such as which markets you analyze and your session preferences.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We use Supabase for authentication and database services.

Cookies & Local Storage

Signals FM uses local storage to save your UI preferences (such as light/dark mode and demo portfolio state). We use session cookies for authentication only — no tracking or advertising cookies.
