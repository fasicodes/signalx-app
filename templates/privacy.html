Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a hashed password, or your name, email and profile picture if you sign in with Google.
  • Exchange API keys, only if you connect the Auto-Trade bot.
  • Trading activity on Signals FM: Demo Trading orders, positions, journal notes and settings; Auto-Trade settings, positions and activity logs; your watchlist and alerts.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser information, used for security and rate limiting.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session active.
  • To run the features you use: signals, Demo Trading, the Auto-Trade bot, alerts and your saved preferences.
  • To send account emails such as verification and password reset.
  • To keep the service secure and to improve it.

How we protect it

Data is stored in our MySQL database with our hosting provider and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Exchange API secrets are encrypted with a server-side key before they are saved, are never shown again, and are deleted as soon as you disconnect.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We use a few service providers to run Signals FM:

  • our hosting and database provider;
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google;
  • Gmail, to deliver account emails;
  • the exchange you connect (for example Binance), which receives the orders the bot places on your behalf;
  • market data providers (OKX, Yahoo Finance), which receive price requests but no personal data;
    • {% if ga_measurement_id %}
  • Google Analytics, to count visits with IP anonymization.

Cookies and local storage

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. Your browser's local storage keeps interface preferences such as the theme, chart timeframe and favorite coins. {% if ga_measurement_id %}Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits.{% else %}We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.{% endif %}

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Auto-Trade activity logs are deleted after 14 days. Demo Trading history is kept so you can review your past practice sessions.

Your choices and rights

You can disconnect exchange keys at any time from the Auto-Trade page, which deletes them. You can ask us to export or delete your account and all associated data.

Children

Signals FM is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
