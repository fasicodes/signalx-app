About us

Crypto signals you can check for yourself

Signals FM is a web-based trading signal platform. It reads the market with tested statistical models and shows every result, wins and losses, so traders can make better decisions with less guesswork.

Who we are

Signals FM is built and run by Fasiullah Ayaz, founder and CEO. It started from a simple problem: before a trade, traders often collect calculations from many different platforms and still are not sure what they mean. Signals FM brings those analytical methods together in one place.

Whether you are just starting your trading journey or already have experience in the markets, Signals FM is designed to give you helpful insight when you want a second opinion on the market.

Our mission

To make trading analysis easier to reach for beginners and experienced traders alike. Instead of comparing numbers from different platforms, you see the market's picture in one place, in plain language, with the risks spelled out.

How the signal engine works

Three rules decide every signal. They are the same rules that were tested before you ever see a signal.

Reads closed 4-hour candles{{ tip('candle_4h', '4-hour candle') }}

The engine only uses candles that have finished. For each of the 30 coins it checks, it reads the coin's own price action, its daily trend, Bitcoin and the market of 16 large coins: 82 measurements in all.

Signals only its strongest setups

A machine-learning model estimates each trade's chance of reaching its target before its stop. Only roughly the top 3% of setups become a Long or Short signal; the rest of the time it says Wait.

Tested on data it never saw{{ tip('unseen_data', 'tested on unseen data') }}

The model learned from January 2022 to June 2025 and was then tested once on July 2025 to October 2026, with every fee included and losses counted the pessimistic way.

What we promise

Every result, not just the wins

The track record logs every signal automatically with its stop, target and result after fees. Nothing is deleted.

No guaranteed profits

No signal service can promise profit, and we never will. We would rather show Wait than a weak trade.

Practice before real money

Demo trading uses live prices and virtual money, so you can learn how signals behave before risking anything.

Your keys stay protected

Exchange keys for the auto-trade bot are encrypted on the server, and keys that allow withdrawals are refused.
