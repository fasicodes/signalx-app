How this is measured

  • Same coins, every candle. BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, ADA and LINK are read at every 4-hour candle close by signal engine v2. Every long or short is logged.
  • Entry is the close of the candle that gave the signal.
  • Stop-loss is 3 × ATR(14) away from entry and the target is 1.5 × ATR(14), exactly as the dashboard shows them. The target is closer than the stop, so a high win rate is needed to break even (about 67% plus fees). Judge the engine by the average R and the equity curve, not by the win rate alone.
  • One signal per coin at a time. New signals are ignored until the open one closes.
  • Pessimistic fills. If one candle touches both the stop and the target, it counts as a loss. If a candle opens past the stop, the exit is that worse price.
  • Time limit. After 48 candles (8 days) without hitting either level, the signal closes at that candle's close.
  • After costs. Every result already has 0.12% of the position taken off for fees and slippage (round trip). Funding payments on futures are not included.
  • Backtest replays the same engine over the last 365 days and is refreshed every week. At each candle the engine only uses data that had closed by then, and the outcome comes only from the candles after it.
  • How the model was checked. The engine was trained on Jan 2022 – Jun 2025 and then tested once on Jul 2025 – Oct 2026, data it had never seen: see below.
