Risk disclosure

Last updated: October 2026

⚠ Please read this first. Most people who trade leveraged crypto products lose money. Only trade with money you can afford to lose completely.

Crypto markets move fast

Crypto prices can rise or fall more than 10% in a single day, and markets trade around the clock. Prices can gap past your stop-loss, liquidity can disappear, and exchanges can go offline exactly when you need them.

Leverage and liquidation

Leverage multiplies both gains and losses. At 10x leverage, a 10% move against you wipes out the margin of the position; at 50x it takes only 2%. With cross margin, a losing position can use up your whole futures balance. Futures also charge or pay funding every few hours, which adds to the cost of holding a position.

Signals are estimates, not predictions

Signals come from statistical models. The confidence score is the model's own estimate, not a guaranteed probability. Signals can be wrong many times in a row. The track record, including the backtest, describes the past and is calculated before fees and slippage; a backtest replays rules on history and can look better than real trading. Neither guarantees future results.

Auto-Trade places real orders

When the Auto-Trade bot is on and connected to a live account, it trades your real money without asking you first. Orders can fail, fill late or fill at worse prices because of exchange outages, network issues, API changes, software errors or server downtime. A stop-loss is not guaranteed to fill at its price. Start with the Demo account, use small sizes, keep withdrawal permission off on your API keys, and check your exchange account regularly.

Demo Trading is a simulation

Demo Trading uses live prices but simulated fills, fees, funding and liquidations. Real trading differs: order-book depth, latency and your own emotions with real money all change results. Good demo results don't guarantee good live results.

Your situation and the law

Nothing on Signals FM considers your personal finances, goals or risk tolerance. Crypto and crypto derivatives are restricted or regulated differently in different countries, and profits may be taxable. Make sure what you do is legal where you live, and get independent professional advice if you are unsure.

Summary

  • Use a stop-loss on every trade and keep risk per trade small (many traders use 0.5% to 2%).
  • Prefer low leverage. High leverage turns normal price noise into liquidations.
  • Practice in Demo Trading before risking real money.
  • Never trade with money you need for living costs or that you've borrowed.
