Welcome to Signals FM

BuySellWait

Our tested models check 102 crypto coins every 4 hours, and 62 of them every hour too. When a coin has a good setup it gives a Buy (price likely to rise) or Sell (price likely to fall) signal; otherwise that coin says Wait.

Coins right now shows the answer for every coin on one page, and Signals shows each signal with its stop-loss and targets.

How to read a signal

Targetwhere you take profit
Entrywhere the signal starts
Stop losswhere you exit if it goes wrong

The bar on the signal card shows where the live price is between the stop and the target. The 1h and 4h signals have one target, as far from the entry as the stop: close the whole trade there. A signal ends at the target, at the stop, or when its time runs out (at most 2 days on 1h signals, 9 days on 4h signals). Experimental signals have three targets: close a third at each, and move the stop to the entry after the first.

Keep every loss small

All 3 targets+1.17R
TP1, then stop at entry+0.17R
Stop before TP1−1R

In testing about 7 in 10 signals closed in profit, but many wins are small and a full loss is −1R, and some months lost money.

So risk a small, fixed amount on every signal, for example 1% of your account. That amount is called 1R.
