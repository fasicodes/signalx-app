Welcome to Signals FM

LongShortWait

Every 4 hours the signal engine checks 30 coins. Most of the time it says Wait. When it finds one of its strongest setups, it gives a Long (price likely to rise) or Short (price likely to fall) signal.

Wait is normal. It means the engine is skipping weak setups to protect you.

How to read a signal

Targetwhere you take the profit
Entrywhere the signal starts
Stop losswhere you exit if it goes wrong

The bar on the signal card shows where the live price is between the stop and the target. A signal ends when it reaches the target, hits the stop, or after 8 days.

Keep every loss small

Win (about 7 in 10)+0.5R
Loss−1R

In testing about 7 in 10 signals won, but each loss was about twice the size of each win, and some months lost money.

So risk a small, fixed amount on every signal, for example 1% of your account. That amount is called 1R.
