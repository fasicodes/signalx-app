Four rules for beginners

  • Risk only 1% of your account on one trade. The risk calculator works out the size for you.
  • Always set the stop-loss. It is the price where the idea was wrong. Close the trade at the target (on three-target signals, a third at each).
  • Keep only 2 or 3 trades open at once. Signals often come in groups because coins move together, so several open trades can win or lose together.
  • No signal wins every time. Practise in demo trading first, and see every win and loss on the track record.
