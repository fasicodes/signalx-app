Read these numbers carefully

  • R is the risk of one trade: the distance from entry to stop. +0.10R per trade means that, on average, a trade made a tenth of what it risked, after costs.
  • Win rate is the share of trades that ended above zero. Trend strategies often win less than half of their trades and still make money with a few big winners; mean-reversion strategies the other way round.
  • This is a plain backtest of public rules over one year. A good year can be luck, so look at the number of trades, the drawdown and how many symbols made money. It is not one of the site's tested signal models and not advice.
