Before you start

Short answers to what beginners ask most.

Do I need trading experience?

No. Every signal tells you where to enter, where to get out if it goes wrong and where to take profit. Start in Demo trading with virtual money, and tap any "?" in the app to see what a word means.

How accurate are the signals?

{% if WIN %}In a year of testing on data the models never saw, {{ WIN }}% of {% if NSIG %}{{ "{:,}".format(NSIG) }} {% endif %}signals reached the target{% if AVGR is not none %}, and the average signal made {{ "%+.2f" | format(AVGR) }}R after fees{% endif %}. {% endif %}Not every signal wins, so keep the risk on each signal small.

Is there a signal on every coin all the time?

No, and that is on purpose. A coin gets a signal only when a model sees a good trade; the others say "Wait". {% if PERDAY %}Across all coins there are about {{ PERDAY }} signals a day on average.{% endif %} Forcing a trade on every coin would lose money.

Can it trade for me?

Yes, if you want. The optional Auto-Trade bot places the signals on your own Binance account, with a stop-loss on every trade, a daily loss limit and a Stop all button. Try Demo trading first.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free during the beta. Paid plans will be announced in advance, and nothing changes without notice.

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are information from a model, for education. Trading can lose money, so only trade what you can afford to lose.
